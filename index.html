Australia, 2011-2021

Who is hurt on Australian roads, where, and is it changing?

Road deaths make headlines. This story looks at the much larger number of people who survive a crash but are admitted to hospital.

1 · The big picture

How did non-fatal hospitalised road traffic injury counts change from 2011 to 2021, and how did the pattern in 2019-2021 compare with earlier years?

Each year, tens of thousands of people are admitted to hospital after a road crash. In 2011 it was about 34,000; by 2021 about 39,500.

Two dashed lines mark changes in how Victoria (2012) and New South Wales (2017) record admissions. Part of the movement around those years reflects recording, not safety.

Counts dipped in 2020 and recovered in 2021. The data cannot show why.
